## ✨ What is this?

A **voice-first shopping list** that works like a real person would:

- You **say** what you want — *"add 2 milks and bread"* — it adds it.
- You **change your mind** — *"remove apples"* — it removes it.
- It **remembers for you** — *"you're running low on milk"* — before you even notice.
- It **suggests helpfully** — seasonal fruits, healthier swaps — never annoyingly.

No typing needed. No complicated menus. Just conversation.

## 🧠 The Approach — How It Thinks Like You Do

We didn’t build a robot that just matches keywords. We built it to **think in layers**, just like humans do:

### 1. ⚡ Fast Thinking *vs* 🤔 Slow Thinking

> Inspired by how your brain works

- **Fast Thinking (Reflex):** When you say something simple like *“add milk”* — it doesn’t overthink. It reacts **instantly**, just like you catching a ball. Super fast, effortless.
- **Slow Thinking (Deliberate):** When you say something tricky like *“add two honeycrisp apples and the oat milk alternative if sugar is out of stock”* — it **pauses, thinks carefully**, and figures out the nuance. Slower, but much smarter.

You’ll actually **see** which one was used — a small badge tells you `⚡ Instant` or `🧠 Thought it through`. It’s transparent, not a black box.

### 2. 🎧 Careful Listening

If your audio is in Hindi ( *“doodh aur andey chahiye”* ) or Spanish ( *“agregar dos leches”* ) or noisy — it switches to a **more careful listening mode** to catch every word correctly.

**In short:** Simple talk = instant. Complex or multilingual talk = careful. You always get the right balance of speed and accuracy.

## 💡 The Logic Behind The Smart Nudges

This is where it gets clever — and human.

We don’t use a mysterious “AI recommender.” We use **explainable, everyday logic** you can actually understand and trust:

### 🥛 1. The Pantry Logic — *“You’re about to run out”*

Like how YOU know milk lasts ~5 days in your fridge:

```
You added 🥛 Milk  →  Assistant remembers: “Milk lasts 5 days”
Day 1 ▓▓▓▓▓░░░░░░  Fresh
Day 4 ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░  Still okay
Day 5 ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓  ⚠️ Running low! Nudge appears
```

Every item has its own “freshness clock”:
- `Bananas` → 4 days 🍌
- `Bread` → 6 days 🍞
- `Eggs` → 14 days 🥚
- `Olive Oil` → 60 days 🫒
- `Rice` → 45 days 🍚

You see a **progress bar** on each item that slowly fills up. When it hits ~80%, it gently surfaces as a *“Running Low”* card. No nagging — only when it makes sense. If you already re-added it and it’s fresh, it won’t bother you.

### ☀️ 2. The Seasonal Logic — *“What’s fresh right now?”*

Like a farmer at the market:

> *“It’s August — mangoes and berries are at their peak and cheapest!”*

It checks the current month and suggests produce that’s **naturally in season right now** — fresher, tastier, and usually on sale. Never suggests what’s already in your cart.

### 🔄 3. The Substitute Logic — *“Try this instead?”*

Like a helpful store clerk:

> *“We’re out of milk? Oat milk is a great alternative.”*
> *“Need sugar? Try honey.”*

It knows sensible swaps:
- `Milk` → `Oat Milk`, `Almond Milk`
- `Sugar` → `Honey`
- `Butter` → `Olive Oil`

Only suggests when relevant to what’s already in your list.

## ❓ Frequently Asked — In Plain Words

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need to speak perfectly?</b></summary>
No. Say <i>“apples”</i> or <i>“apple”</i>, <i>“apply”</i> or <i>“apples”</i> — it understands plurals, typos, and sound-alikes. It even matches <i>“apple”</i> to <i>“Organic Honeycrisp Apples”</i>.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What if I tap mic by mistake and say nothing?</b></summary>
It will politely tell you <i>“No speech detected. Tap the mic, say your item, and tap again.”</i> Nothing gets added randomly.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I edit quantity or category?</b></summary>
Yes! On each item you can <b>+ / –</b> quantity and change category (like Dairy, Produce) from a dropdown. Or just say <i>“remove 2 milks”</i> to reduce.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Will it spam me with suggestions?</b></summary>
Never. It only shows 3 types of cards when useful: <b>Running Low</b> (near depletion), <b>Seasonal</b> (this month’s best), <b>Substitutes</b> (for items you already have). You can dismiss any card with ✕.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it work without internet voice?</b></summary>
Yes — for English you can speak or type and it works instantly in your browser. For Hindi/Spanish or noisy audio, it uses a more careful listening mode when online.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where is my list stored?</b></summary>
Safely on your device’s list — you can add, remove, clear anytime. Refresh the page and your list stays.
</details>
